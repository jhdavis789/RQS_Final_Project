## Verdict

**v2 is a substantial, clear improvement on scientific grounds. Confidence: high.** Every one of the six CRITICAL defects that the scientific-accuracy critique flagged in v1 is either fixed or converted from a falsifiable error into a defensible hedge, and most of the SIGNIFICANT and MINOR items are also addressed. The thesis now sits inside a proper Adaptive Markets Hypothesis meta-frame (slide 2); the Kandasamy chart no longer fabricates a middle bar; the DDM "theorem" has become a Bayesian prediction with correct co-citation of the 2016 *Neuron* and *Neural Computation* papers; the R²=0.55 claim is gone; the lPAG label is anatomically correct; the Coates chart is now a within-trader schematic with the paper's actual reported statistics (r²=0.40; 68% cortisol rise in one representative trader); the "biomarker" overclaim has become "consistent with"; the "four independent substrates" claim is now "four levels of description"; the HTML `</h2>` bug at line 753 is cleanly fixed (v2 uses `</div>`); the 5-vs-6 disciplines inconsistency is reconciled; and the new slide 13 bond-market translation finally makes the deck bond-native. v2 is not perfect — the PFC/PAG functional-analog caveat is handled well but still inherits some of the rhetorical load, and several appendix slides the skeptic demanded are still unwritten placeholders — but the drop from "will be caught by a sharp neuroscientist in real time" to "will survive Q&A if the appendix is built out" is real and measurable.

## Top 5 changes to prioritize for v3

1. **Add an NLP validation slide to the main deck, not just the appendix.** Prompt, model + version, held-out inter-coder κ, correlation with a blind behavioral outcome (conviction rating, position size, or turnover), and a temporal-drift check across the 2018–2024 window. This closes skeptic #1 and shores up the entire empirical stack. Without it the deck is one question away from collapse no matter how clean the science layer reads.
2. **Add a multiple-comparisons / pre-registration slide before the data slides.** Which hypotheses were pre-registered, which discovered; FWER/BH-corrected p-values; honest accounting of how many per-analyst sub-analyses were run. Closes skeptic #2.
3. **Fix the two citation problems: Greenwood-Hanson 2013 *RFS* (not *JF*), and verify or replace the Ma-Paligorova-Peydró 2021 reference.** Both are on the new bond-market slide and an RQS bond quant will catch the journal error.
4. **Add effect size, p-value, and N to the hook slide.** "Statistically reliable shortening" needs a number — even a preliminary z-score or p from a pooled test. The skeptic's point stands: a numerical anchor on slide 1 signals "numbers drive narrative" rather than the reverse.
5. **Add a SCHEMATIC watermark on the hook chart and on the Fleming scatter.** Both v2 charts read as "data" even though the source labels say schematic. A literal `SCHEMATIC` overlay or diagonal wash prevents the 5-second-glance misread that a sharp audience member won't verbalize but will subtract points for.

(Runner-up: an actual backtested Sharpe-uplift for rec 2 tail-weighted sizing, even with a wide CI. The current "before implementation, back-test" hedge is defensible but leaves the audience with a recommendation whose magnitude is unknown.)
