## Top-level thesis lines
1. **"Behavioral biases have measurable biological signatures. Once we can measure them, we can build instruments around them that improve outcomes."**
2. **"Self-awareness is helpful but it is not the lever. Process is."** *(Backed by knowledge/06: Morewedge effect sizes + Scopelliti 2015 showing high-bias-blind-spot subjects benefit least from training.)*
3. **"When five independent fields predict the same thing and our data shows it, the conclusion is not single-field coincidence."**

## Analyst confidence — A1a (knowledge/01, 09)
5. **"Per-analyst normalization isn't statistical convenience — it's the only way to recover a cross-analyst signal from a measurement that varies by genotype, neuroanatomy, prior, and writing style."**
6. **"16-34% of overconfidence is heritable. Hyland's writer-style is stable across topics. Different analyst, different posterior precision parameter — comparing absolute confidence is comparing different scales."**

---

## Confidence extremes — A1b (knowledge/02, 07)
7. **"The U-shape is not an artifact. It is a theorem. Drift-diffusion models with extreme posteriors arising from clean accumulation predict accuracy is U-shaped in reported confidence — Sanders, Hangya, Kepecs proved it formally."**
8. **"The middle of an analyst's confidence is noise. The tails carry the alpha. Sizing should weight accordingly."**

## 2022 horizon compression — A1c (knowledge/03, 04, 07, 08, 11)
9. **"The 2022 analyst note is functioning as a non-invasive biomarker of an HPA episode."**
10. **"Cortisol *causally* shrinks the future. Kandasamy 2014 — eight days of cortisol elevation, utility curvature flips, certainty-equivalent risk premium collapses 44%. Not a correlation, an experiment."**
11. **"Schwabe and Wolf showed the goal-to-habit shift is *blocked by propranolol*. The mechanism is noradrenergic. We can stop arguing about whether it's real."**

## PM trades in stress — B1 (knowledge/04, 09, 10)
12. **"Active-risk-up is fight. Active-risk-down is flight. No-trade is freeze. Each maps to a specific neural circuit. The decomposition isn't a metaphor."**
13. **"In Coates' 2009 PNAS data, 2D:4D digit ratio explained R² = 0.553 of variance in trader profitability. Eleven-times P&L spread between top and bottom tertiles. Biological individual differences are not a rounding error."**
14. **"Mobbs 2007 *Science* — the brain switches from PFC-mediated cognitive appraisal to PAG-mediated reflexive response as threat proximity rises. We can see it in the fMRI. PMs in a flash drawdown are in the second regime."**

---

## Brain-state reconfiguration (knowledge/08)
15. **"Acute stress reconfigures the *whole brain*, not just one region. Hermans 2011 *Science* — salience network up, executive control down — and propranolol blocks it but cortisol doesn't, isolating the noradrenergic mechanism."**
16. **"Heart-rate variability indexes prefrontal-vagal control. Pupillometry indexes locus-coeruleus drive. State-of-brain is measurable in real time with off-the-shelf wearables."**

---

## Market-level read (knowledge/05)
17. **"If our decisions degrade in stress and the market's do too, the only relevant question is whether our edge holds up on the *relative* scorecard."**
18. **"Coates' aggregation argument is the missing link: correlated endocrine responses on a trading floor produce bubbles and crashes. Micro-to-macro mechanism, not metaphor."**
19. **"Adaptive Markets is the right meta-frame for an RQS audience — it preserves quantitative rigor and explains regime-dependent efficiency without throwing out either EMH or behavioral finance."**

---

## Debiasing — defending the thesis (knowledge/06)
20. **"Morewedge 2015 is the strongest pro-self-awareness evidence: bias reduced ~30% immediately, ~20-24% at three months — but only with purpose-built training. Off-the-shelf 'bias awareness' is not the same intervention."**
21. **"Scopelliti 2015: people high on the bias-blind-spot scale benefit *least* from training. Our most senior people are exactly the population the lab evidence says training works least well on."**
22. **"Haynes 2009 surgical checklist cut mortality by 47%. Process *can* work. Self-awareness usually doesn't, especially under stress, especially in experts."**
23. **"Kahneman's late-career view: organizations beat individuals at debiasing. Build the instrument; don't ask the human to be different."**

---

## Sleep and chronobiology (knowledge/11)
24. **"Yoo 2007 — sleep loss amplifies amygdala reactivity by 60% and decouples it from PFC. The neural signature of a sleep-deprived analyst is the neural signature of an acutely stressed one. 2022 was both."**
25. **"The Kamstra-Kramer-Levi daylight-saving-time anomaly is the cleanest population-level evidence that sleep moves prices."**
26. **"Sleep policy is the cheapest, highest-leverage lever in this entire deck."**

---

## Evolutionary frame (knowledge/10)
27. **"Johnson and Fowler proved overconfidence is the evolutionarily stable strategy when contests have asymmetric payoffs. The fight response in our data is not a bug — it's a feature, deployed in the wrong environment."**
28. **"Loss aversion comes from threshold-bearing fitness. Our ancestors who weren't loss-averse died. The bias isn't irrational, it's evolutionarily adaptive — for ancestral resource scarcity, not for quarterly drawdowns."**
29. **"Gigerenzer is right that heuristics can be ecologically rational. Kahneman is right that they misfire in modern markets. Both are true. The lever is matching environment to heuristic — process design."**

---

## Implementation (knowledge/04, 06, 08, 11)
30. **"PFC capacity is degraded exactly when self-awareness would need to fire. The instrument has to be in place *before* the stress, not summoned during it."**
31. **"Four instruments, each independently grounded in peer-reviewed mechanism *and* our own data: per-analyst normalization, tail-weighted sizing, stress-window protocol, sleep policy."**
32. **"None of these instruments depends on people being less biased. They absorb known biases as design parameters."**

---

## Closing line
33. **"Three biases. Five disciplines converge. Four instruments. Measure → instrument → decide."**

## Q&A landmines (with rehearsed responses)
- *"Are you measuring confidence or just verbal style?"* → Per-analyst normalization handles writer-style heterogeneity (Hyland, Biber). Validated against [LLM-confidence prior art: Loughran-McDonald, Kim 2024]. Knowledge/01.
- *"Could this just be small-N / luck?"* → Family-wise error rate handled in appendix; per-individual robustness shown for each finding. Knowledge file 03 covers 2022 vs other stress regimes.
- *"Why should I trust pop-neuroscience in my investment process?"* → This is not pop-neuroscience. Five independent peer-reviewed disciplines converge on the same prediction. (Cross-disciplinary convergence — talking point #4.)
- *"Isn't the solution to train analysts to be more self-aware?"* → Morewedge effect sizes + Scopelliti blind-spot moderation + Kahneman *Noise* (knowledge/06). Process beats self-awareness, especially for senior people under stress (knowledge/04: PFC offline at exactly the moment self-awareness needs to fire).
- *"Can we screen analysts/PMs for these biological markers?"* → Hard ethical/legal limits. What IS actionable: self-knowledge tools, calibrated stress-window protocols. Knowledge/09.
- *"Is 2022 special enough to base a recommendation on?"* → Robustness across other stress windows (1994, 2008, 2013, 2020) — placeholder for empirical confirmation.
- *"What's the incremental lift over our existing tools?"* → Measurement instruments are additive, not replacement. They become inputs to existing tools.
