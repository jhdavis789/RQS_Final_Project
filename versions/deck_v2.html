Thesis 02 / 15
The claim

Behavioral biases have measurable biological signatures. Once we can measure them, we can build instruments around them that improve outcomes.

Meta-frame: Adaptive Markets Hypothesis (Lo 2004). Markets are efficient in regime-dependent ways. Both rational-frictions mechanisms (intermediary balance sheet, liquidity) and behavioral mechanisms (stress-driven horizon compression, conviction at the tails) show up in prices. The question is which, in which regime, for what instrument — and that is the quant's job, not a battle between EMH and behavioral finance.
Framework · Evidence at multiple levels of description 03 / 15
Three experimental findings · five disciplinary vocabularies

The same underlying HPA–PFC pathway, described at five levels, tested in our data

This is not five independent bets on independent hypotheses. It is one causal pathway — HPA / catecholamines / PFC / metacognitive circuits — described at five levels of analysis, each with failure modes, and tested against our data at the behavioral scale. Mechanistic integration, not statistical convergence. The argument is stronger, not weaker, for saying so.
A1a · Science · Metacognition 04 / 15
Experiment A1a · Heterogeneous analyst confidence

Metacognitive set-points are stable per person — genetics, neuroanatomy, language, and computation all point the same way

Anterior PFC grey-matter volume correlates with metacognitive accuracy
Fleming et al. 2010, Science 329:1541 · VBM study, N=32 healthy adults · significant cluster in right BA10
Schematic scatter reproducing the reported cluster-level positive correlation. The paper uses voxel-based morphometry with meta-d′/d′ as the metacognitive index.
Per-analyst confidence distributions are incomparable on an absolute scale
Writer-level hedge/booster style is stable across topics (Hyland; Biber stance corpora)
Conceptual illustration. Actual per-analyst distributions from our corpus appear on the next (data) slide.
Four levels of description of a single stable individual difference — not four independent substrates: genetic (Cesarini et al. 2009, Swedish twin study — 16–34% additive-genetic variance in overprecision), neural (Fleming et al. 2010 — right aPFC grey-matter volume, with causal confirmation from Kepecs 2008 / Lak 2014 OFC confidence coding), behavioral (Hyland–Biber — hedge/booster style is stable per writer across topics), computational (Bayesian frame — confidence is the precision of the posterior; different priors give different reports for identical evidence).
Per-analyst normalization — percentile-within-analyst or analyst-random-effects — is required to recover the biological signal. Absolute confidence levels compare different scales. The specific subtype being measured is overprecision (Moore & Healy 2008), the most stable of the three overconfidence components.

A1b · Science · Signal at the tails 06 / 15
Experiment A1b · Where the alpha concentrates

Under a Bayesian-calibrated reporter, signal concentrates at the tails of the confidence distribution — a prediction, not a theorem

Predicted forward-return signal by per-analyst confidence decile
Signal-to-noise: the middle is where evidence is ambiguous; the tails are where the posterior is well-resolved
Schematic U-shape. The empirical curve from our data appears on the paired data slide. Theoretical basis: signal-detection theory — calibrated extreme posteriors arise from clean evidence accumulation (Sanders, Hangya & Kepecs 2016 Neuron; Hangya, Sanders & Kepecs 2016 Neural Computation).
OFC neurons encode graded confidence — Kepecs "X-pattern"
Firing rate diverges with difficulty depending on trial outcome · rodent OFC · replicated in primate (Masset et al. 2020 Cell) · causal via OFC inactivation (Lak et al. 2014 Neuron)
Schematic of Kepecs et al. 2008 Nature 455:227 Fig. 3c. Lines cross as difficulty increases — confidence discrimination is sharpest at easy and hardest-trial extremes.
Four convergent lines of evidence for the tail-signal phenotype: mathematical (signal-detection / Bayesian posterior is sharpest when evidence is strong either way), cellular (OFC X-pattern, causally validated by OFC inactivation), expert calibration across domains (weather forecasters — Murphy & Winkler; bridge experts — Keren; intelligence analysts — Mandel & Barnes 2014 PNAS; superforecasters — Tetlock), active management (Cremers & Petajisto Active Share; Cohen, Polk & Silli Best Ideas — conviction premium has the same signature in fund returns).
If analysts behave as approximately Bayesian reporters, their confidence-return curve should be U-shaped — the empirical presence of that U is the behavioral signature of calibration. Analysts for whom the U-shape does not appear may need individualized diagnosis.

A1c · Science · Stress physiology 08 / 15
Experiment A1c · 2022 horizon compression

In a controlled experiment, chronic cortisol flattens utility — the brain-mechanism reading of drawdown horizon collapse

Arnsten inverted-U: PFC function collapses under catecholamine surge
Arnsten 2009, 2015 Nat Rev Neurosci · cellular mechanism of stress-impaired deliberative control
Schematic of the consensus inverted-U in Arnsten (2009) Nat Rev Neurosci 10:410 and Arnsten (2015) Nat Neurosci 18:1376. Causal mechanism isolated via β-blocker propranolol blocking stress-induced goal→habit shift (Schwabe et al. 2011 J Neurosci).
Kandasamy 2014: 8 days of cortisol elevation collapses risk-taking by ~44%
Kandasamy et al. 2014 PNAS 111:3608 · N=36 placebo-controlled · chronic-only effect (acute cortisol: P=0.328, n.s.)
Utility curvature α fell from 0.50 (placebo) to 0.35 (8-day hydrocortisone); P=0.022. Certainty-equivalent of the experimental lottery fell from £25 to £14 (−44%). Acute cortisol had no significant effect on α.
Cross-level chain from drawdown to shrinking horizons: endocrinology (Kandasamy — pharmacological cortisol causally flattens utility in controlled lab conditions), cellular neuroscience (Arnsten — catecholamine surge impairs PFC; Schwabe & Wolf — goal→habit shift blocked by propranolol, isolating the noradrenergic mechanism), computational (steepened temporal discounting maps onto aberrant precision on threat priors — also falsifiable against a rational-Bayesian-volatility-update account), sleep (Yoo et al. 2007 — sleep loss amplifies amygdala BOLD response ~60% and decouples it from PFC; drawdown stress likely compounds).
The 2022 horizon compression is consistent with the behavioral signature of HPA-axis-driven PFC impairment documented under cortisol dosing (Kandasamy 2014). This is a hypothesis to be tested by paired text + cortisol/HRV collection — not yet a validated biomarker.

B1 · Science · Defensive-response circuits 10 / 15
Experiment B1 · PM trades under volatility

Active-risk-up · benchmark-hug · no-trade map to functionally distinct circuits — a useful decomposition, not a direct circuit read-out

Defensive output columns of the periaqueductal gray
Bandler & Shipley 1994 TINS 17:379 · Mobbs et al. 2007 Science 317:1079 PFC→PAG switch as threat gets proximal
Schematic after Bandler & Shipley 1994 (four PAG columns with their behavioral outputs) and Mobbs et al. 2007 (cognitive appraisal → reflexive midbrain as threat imminence rises). Fight/flight/freeze are the functional outputs; PM-level behaviors are their analogs, not direct circuit read-outs.
Cortisol tracks market stress in professional traders
Coates & Herbert 2008 PNAS 105:6167 · 17 London equity-derivative traders · 8-day within-trader panel · correlation vs. implied vol
Schematic of the within-trader relationship reported in Coates & Herbert 2008. Paper reports cortisol-variance vs. P&L-variance r²=0.40 (P=0.007); one representative trader's afternoon cortisol rose ~68% as implied vol rose from ~0.5% to ~0.8% in a week. Sample is male equity-derivative HFT — translation to bond PMs is a proposed extension, not a demonstrated finding.
Four behavioral signatures, four neural substrates, one practical decomposition: fight (approach / aggression / active risk) — dorsal PAG columns, testosterone-mediated (Coates 2009 PNAS — prenatal-androgen markers correlate with trader risk-taking in a 2009 HFT sample; this finding's effect size has not fully replicated in broader populations and should be treated as a first-generation signal); flight (avoidance / benchmark-hug) — dorsolateral PAG, cortisol-mediated; freeze / passive coping (no trade) — ventrolateral PAG, parasympathetic shutdown; confrontation (aggressive counter-positioning) — lateral PAG. Under acute stress, Mobbs' MRI work shows the brain shifts from vmPFC cognitive appraisal to PAG reflexive programs as threat imminence rises.
The functional decomposition (approach / avoid / no-trade / counter) has a neural analog — it is a useful causal map for instrument design, not a claim that benchmark-hugging is a dorsolateral-PAG read-out. The lever is environment design, not willpower.

Bridging · Brain-state reconfiguration and biomarkers 12 / 15
Why self-awareness can't carry the work — and what can be measured

Acute stress reconfigures whole-brain networks. Some of that reconfiguration is readable with off-the-shelf hardware; much of it is not.

Hermans 2011 Science: the "salience hijack"
Acute stress → salience network up, central executive down · propranolol blocks the reconfiguration; metyrapone (cortisol blocker) does not
Schematic after Hermans, van Marle et al. 2011 Science 334:1151 and Hermans et al. 2014 Trends in Neurosciences 37:304. Propranolol/metyrapone dissociation isolates the noradrenergic (not cortisol-glucocorticoid) driver of the fast-phase reconfiguration.
Heart-rate variability as a candidate decision-state biomarker
Thayer & Lane 2000; Thayer 2009 Ann Behav Med — HRV and cognitive-performance meta-analytic r ≈ 0.2–0.3
Schematic of the neurovisceral-integration relationship. There is no published continuous HRV + pupil + market-telemetry evidence in professional PMs; translating basic-science HRV–cognition effect sizes to investment-decision quality is an open research frontier, not a shovel-ready deployment.
Two stress timescales, two measurable axes: fast noradrenergic (0–30 min, relevant to flash drawdowns; indexable via pupil dilation — Joshi et al. 2016 Neuron — and short-term HRV changes), slow cortisol (60+ min, relevant to multi-day stress; indexable via salivary/hair cortisol and HRV trend). The strongest honest claim: the measurement technology is mature in basic-science contexts. The translation evidence to PM telemetry is an open frontier — and Capital Group is positioned to produce the first-mover evidence with a paired-biometric pilot.
Under acute stress the prefrontal cortex is offline — the circuit that would implement "just be self-aware" is exactly the circuit that fails. Instruments must be in place before stress, not summoned during it.

Recommendations 14 / 15
Four instruments grounded in mechanism and in our own data

Measurement first. Then instruments that absorb known biases as design parameters.

01 · From A1a
Per-analyst confidence normalization in PM tools
Surface percentile-within-analyst or analyst-random-effect-adjusted confidence, not absolute level. The biological signal (metacognitive prior precision) is only recoverable on the relative scale — supported by genetic, neural, behavioral, and computational-level evidence converging on stable per-person set-points.
02 · From A1b
Tail-weighted sizing bands for analyst conviction
Signal-detection theory predicts a U-shape in accuracy vs. confidence for calibrated reporters. Size conviction with weight on the tails of each analyst's own historical distribution. Before implementation: back-test expected Sharpe uplift on the internal panel with an honest confidence interval.
03 · From A1c + B1
Stress-window decision protocol
Cooling-off gates, structured premortem (Klein), and pre-committed checklists triggered on bright-line VIX / drawdown / realized-vol thresholds. Rationale: PFC capacity is degraded exactly when self-awareness would need to fire (Arnsten). Process-level evidence: Pronovost 2006 central-line infection reduction (~66%) is more replicable than Haynes 2009 checklist; implementation fidelity is the binding constraint (Urbach 2014 null in Ontario rollout).
04 · From chronobiology
Sleep & scheduling policy around high-stakes decisions
Yoo 2007: sleep loss amplifies amygdala BOLD response ~60% and weakens amygdala–PFC functional connectivity — the neural signature closely overlaps with acute-stress effects. Proposal: opt-in wearable pilot (N≈20 PMs, pre-registered pre-FOMC windows) to establish a within-Capital-Group sleep × decision-quality effect size before any policy lever.
None of these four instruments requires people to be less biased. They absorb known biases as design parameters — the form of debiasing with the most durable evidence base in expert populations (Kahneman, Sibony & Sunstein 2021, Noise). They are meant to layer with existing analyst training, not replace it.
